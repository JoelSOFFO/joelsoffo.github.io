Joel Soffo

I am currently a third year PhD student at Airbus and Centrale Nantes (Laboratoire LMJL), under the supervision of Prof. Anthony Nouy (Centrale Nantes) and Antoine Bensalah (Airbus). My field of research is in data-driven methods for solving Partial Differential Equations (PDEs), also known as Scientific Machine Learning (SciML). My research interest includes approaches such as nonlinear model reduction techniques (dimensionality reduction), and operator learning methods. I am also currently interested in Gradient Descent methods (especially Natural Gradient Descent) and optimal sampling strategies. This field is of main interest in many engineering tasks, as the goal is to accelerate the computation, compared to classic numerical solvers. Feel free to reach if you want to discuss these topics, or any Machine Learning related topic in general. I'm also an amateur tennis player, always looking for a new partner !
