ABOUT ME

Here is my CV

I am currently a PhD student at Airbus and Centrale Nantes (Laboratoire LMJL), under the supervision of Prof. Anthony Nouy at Centrale Nantes and Antoine Bensalah at Airbus. My field of research is in data-driven methods for solving physics problems (Scientific Machine Learning). My research interest includes approaches such as model reduction techniques, and deep learning algorithms for operator learning. My current focus is also in leveraging Gradient Descent algorithms (especially Natural Gradient Descent) and optimal sampling strategies for real-time applications.
Before this, I was a recipient of the Sophie Germain scholarship granted by the FMJH, and completed my Master's degree in Data Science with highest honors, at Institut Polytechnique de Paris and Université Paris-Saclay.

Research interests: Deep learning, dimensionality reduction, Physics-informed ML, Operator learning, Gradient Descent.

I am also an amateur tennis player 🎾. Hit me up if you are in Paris area and want to share the court !
